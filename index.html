02 / About Us
Background & approach
15+
Years of Professional Experience

A practice built around images, ideas and identity.

I am a graphic designer and brand consultant with more than fifteen years of professional experience across design studios, agencies and a range of industries.

My work spans visual identity, packaging, printing and communication design — bringing together concept, visual language and practical production requirements.

Alongside my design practice, I am developing an academic path in psychology. This ongoing interest informs the way I think about visual culture, identity, perception and human behavior.

Good design is not only what we see. It is how an idea becomes clear, meaningful and recognizable.
