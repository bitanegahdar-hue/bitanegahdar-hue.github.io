About Me

I am a graphic designer and photographer with more than 15 years of professional experience working with advertising agencies and organizations across various industries, including construction, beauty, and food.

Throughout my professional career, I have been particularly interested in the connection between art, communication, and understanding people. My studies in management, along with my experience working with different brands and audiences, gradually deepened my interest in understanding human behavior, individual needs, and the decision-making process.

For this reason, I expanded my academic and professional interests toward psychology, human-centered fields, and counseling. I am currently taking relevant courses and participating in educational workshops in these areas.
